# AI Marketing Analytics Assistant

Ask questions about marketing campaign performance in plain English.

- **Campaigns and segments:** "Which segment performed best?", "Compare to CMP-2026-003"
- **Customers:** "Which countries had the most converters?" (the SQL used is shown with the answer)
- **Charts:** "Chart revenue by segment"
- **Definitions:** "Define customer segments"

Pick the campaign with the settings button next to the message box. Answers are generated by AI from the
campaign data; check the numbers and the SQL shown before relying on them.
