Customer Segmentation — Executive Summary
Prepared for: VP of Marketing  •  599 customers, DVD rental base


Executive Summary

I created a rules-based lifecycle segmentation framework using recency, engagement, and customer value to identify customers for winback, upsell, reactivation, and retention strategies

Customer Porfolio Snapshot 
Total Customers ......599
Total Revenue ........$61,312
Avg Revenue...........$102.36
Revenue at Risk.......45.3%

Key Findings
●	45.3% ($27,789) of total revenue — sits with customers who have already lapsed or are trending toward it.
●	The best retention dollar isn’t on the Win-Back list. Churn Watchlist customers (94 people, $12,147 in revenue) are above-average in value and haven’t lapsed yet — the highest-return group to reach before they become a 	   	costlier win-back problem.
●	Champions (18 customers) generate 47% more revenue per customer than the base average — a small, high-value group worth protecting and mobilizing for referrals, not just retaining.
●	Engagement Growth is the largest segment (169 customers, 28.2% of the base) but the lowest-value — warrants scaled, automated tactics rather than high-touch investment.
