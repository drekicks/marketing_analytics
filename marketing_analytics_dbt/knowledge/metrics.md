# Marketing Metrics

## Absolute Lift

**Definition:**
Difference between test and control conversion rates,
expressed in percentage points.

**Formula:** 
(test_conversion_rate - control_conversion_rate) * 100

**Available in:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

**Business Purpose:**
Measures incremental conversion performance associated
with the campaign treatment.

## Conversion Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of the applicable audience that converted.

**Formula:**  
Conversions / Audience

**Available In:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

### Test Conversion Rate
Conversion rate calculated using customers assigned to the TEST group.

### Control Conversion Rate
Conversion rate calculated using customers assigned to the CONTROL group.

### Overall Segment Conversion Rate
Conversion rate calculated across the full segment audience, including both TEST and CONTROL customers.

## Revenue Per Customer

**Definition:**  
Average revenue generated per customer in the applicable audience

**Formula:**  
Revenue / Audience

**Available In:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

### Test Revenue Per Customer
Revenue for each customer in the TEST segment.

### Control Revenue Per Customer
Revenue for each customer in the CONTROL segment.

### Overall Segment Revenue Per Customer
Revenue for each customer in the segment.

## Expected Revenue

**Definition:** 
Test-group revenue expected to be generated based on control-group performance

**Formula:**  
control_revenue_per_customer * test_audience

**Available In:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

**Business Purpose:**
Measures expected revenue associated with the campaign.

## Incremental Revenue

**Definition:**  
Additional revenue generated by the campaign.

**Formula:**  
test_revenue-expected_revenue

**Available In:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

**Business Purpose:**
Measures incremental revenue associated
with the campaign.

## Marketing ROI

**Definition:**  
Return on investment (ROI) for the campaign.

**Formula:**  
(incremental_revenue – total_campaign_cost)/total_campaign_cost

**Available In:**
- `segment_performance_summary`

**Business Purpose:**
Measures ROI associated with the campaign.

## Campaign Revenue

**Definition:**  
Revenue attributed to a marketing campaign.

**Formula:**  
Sum of customer-level campaign revenue.

### Test Revenue
Total revenue for the TEST group.

### Control Revenue
Total revenue for the CONTROL group.

### Overall Revenue 
Total revenue for the campaign.

**Available In:**  
- `campaign_performance_summary` — campaign-level total
- `segment_performance_summary` — campaign + segment level
- `customer_analytic_layer` — customer + campaign level

## Offer Cost

**Definition:**  
Cost of a marketing offer. This cost is only associated with customers in TEST group who converted.

**Formula:**  
Sum of customer-level offer costs where conversion_flag = 'Y' and treatment_group = 'TEST'.

**Available in:**
- `segment_performance_summary` — campaign + segment level
- `customer_analytic_layer` — customer + campaign level

## Contact Cost

**Definition:**  
Cost of a marketing contact. This cost is only associated with contacts that were part of the campaign.

**Formula:**  
Sum of customer-level contact costs.

**Available in:**
- `segment_performance_summary` — campaign + segment level
- `customer_analytic_layer` — customer + campaign level

## Total Campaign Cost

**Definition:**  
Total cost of a marketing campaign.

**Formula:**  
contact_cost + offer_cost

**Available in:**
- `campaign_performance_summary` — campaign-level total
- `segment_performance_summary` — campaign + segment level
- `customer_analytic_layer` — customer + campaign level

## Conversions

**Definition:**  
Number of customers who converted during the campaign.

**Formula:**  
Sum of customer-level conversions where conversion_flag = 'Y'.

### Test Conversions
Total conversions in the TEST segment.

### Control Conversions
Total conversions in the CONTROL segment.

### Overall Conversions
Total conversions in the campaign.

**Available in:**
- `campaign_performance_summary` — campaign-level total
- `segment_performance_summary` — campaign + segment level
