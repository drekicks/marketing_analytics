1. This database contains details about a DVD rental business which can be broken out into 5 categories:
	
	-Film details - information about the film and DVD such as description, release_year and rental rate
	-Rental details - information such as rental date and inventory along with customers who rented the DVD
	-Employee details - information about staff who work for the company and the store at which they work
	-Customer details - information about customers who rented the DVD such as contact details and payment information
	-Actor details - information about the actors starring in the films
	
2. The customers come from over 100 countries. Over 50% of the customers come from 10 countries: India, China, United States, Japan, Mexico, Brazil, Russia, Philippines, Indonesia and Turkey.

4. The company purchases DVDs and rents them out for a specific duration and rate based on the film.
